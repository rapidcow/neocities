             === ON INFORMATION, TRUST, AND TECHNOLOGY ===
                      February 12, 2025 | rapidcow

   Over the last few days I have been thinking about the way my
   university operates -- that somehow, just about in every way,
   technology was invented to control us, the students.  I find
   a twisted kind of amusement at the thought of a course that,
   in desperate hopes of soliciting student attendance, hides
   its quizzes (whose answers are solely imparted during lecture)
   after you have taken them:  It couldn't trust its students to
   use the knowledge for the sheer cause of curiosity over the
   fear of using that power to help others cheat the quizzes and
   pass the course without actually attending.

   I thought, as I walked out of the class room, the names of
   those pieces that I didn't write down.  The fluttering snow
   flew by as the face-numbing wind blew coldly.  For long I've
   been a witness of the passage of time and the bits and pieces
   it blew away.  Like the hundreds upon thousands of snowflakes,
   darting into the horizon, blending with the landscape and never
   to be seen again.  Among the snowflakes were the scant bits I
   cared; yet somehow, the thought that this was a piece made un-
   accessible by deliberate design made it feel more uneasy to me.

   A race
