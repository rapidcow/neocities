  • Ravel ``Forlane'' [*]

  • Totally random but Mozart's String Quartet No.19 KV 465 is not what you'd expect Mozart would write (not that he couldn't, but man is it dark. (and the last place you'd expect to hear the DSCH motif (a teacher once told me they listen to Mozart because it's uplifting unlike Chopin, oh boy do I have news for her :] ...)))
  • Liszt S.541 [*] (all of them, especially the second -- not just the third!) and more lyrical Liszt I cannot recall at the moment. (Consolations? dunno lol...)

  • Fauré (whose name I kept misspelling) Op.23 No.1, ``Les berceuax'' (specifically cello not vocal either! like Mischa Maisky) -- also Barcarolle 1 [*]
